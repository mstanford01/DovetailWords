Dovetail Words Android Support

Dovetail Words is a crossword puzzle without clues. Place every two-letter piece so every row and column becomes a real word.

How do I rotate a piece?

Tap a piece to turn it through four arrangements. It can read forward or backward and can be placed across or down.

Can I move a piece after placing it?

Yes. Tap it to rotate it or drag it to a different open position. You can also move it back to the piece area.

How do I know a word is correct?

Completed words light up and receive a checkmark. The puzzle is solved when every row and column forms the intended word.

How do hints and stars work?

Each puzzle begins with three available stars. A hint places one correct piece and lowers the number of stars available for that puzzle. Lifetime stars unlock additional color styles.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Gameplay, puzzle generation, Daily Puzzle, puzzle codes, definitions, sound, statistics, history, and the tutorial work locally.

How does Android backup work?

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may privately back up lasting progress to your Google account. The developer cannot access it. An unfinished puzzle is not included. Backup and restore are managed in your Android or Google backup settings.

Why did my progress disappear after reinstalling?

Android can restore progress only when a suitable device backup exists. Backup timing and restore availability are controlled by Android and your Google account settings.

How do I turn sound off?

Open Settings in Dovetail Words and turn off Sound Effects. The preference is saved for future sessions.

What should I include in a support message?

Please include your Android device model, Android version, Dovetail Words version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot is helpful for visual issues, but remove personal notifications first.
