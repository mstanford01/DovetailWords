Dovetail Words Privacy Policy

Android version
Effective August 24, 2026

The short version: Dovetail Words has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated servers. The developer does not collect or sell personal information. Game data is stored locally. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may privately back up selected progress to your Google account.

Developer and privacy contact

Dovetail Words is developed by Matthew Stanford. Privacy questions can be sent to mstanford01@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores gameplay information locally, including puzzle progress, settings, stars, milestones, unlocked styles, statistics, solve history, daily streaks, completed puzzle codes, and words collected from completed puzzles. This information is used only to provide the game's features.

Android backup and device transfer

Dovetail Words participates in Android's built-in backup system. If backup is enabled in your Android or Google settings, Android may store durable game progress in the private backup associated with your Google account and restore it after reinstalling the app or moving to another device.

The backed-up information may include settings, stars, milestones, unlocks, statistics, solve history, daily progress, completed puzzle codes, and the word collection. An unfinished active puzzle is excluded.

The developer does not use the Google Drive API, cannot view or manage your private Android backup, and does not receive the backed-up information. Google controls backup transmission, encryption, retention, restore, and deletion under your Google account and Android backup settings.

Sharing a result

Sharing happens only after you choose Share. Challenge creates a spoiler-free card and playable puzzle code without solution letters. My Result may include the completed board, puzzle words, solve time, stars, move count, and daily status, but it never includes a playable puzzle code. The app then opens Android's standard sharing chooser. Dovetail Words does not send this information to the developer. The destination you choose handles it under that service's own terms and privacy policy.

Permissions and device data

Dovetail Words requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo, storage, notification, or advertising permission. It does not access the Android Advertising ID. It does not track activity across apps or websites.

Internet access and external services

The app declares no internet permission. Puzzles, Daily Puzzle, puzzle codes, dictionary definitions, statistics, history, sound, and the tutorial work locally. Android's operating-system backup service is the only cloud-backed behavior.

Data collection and sale

The developer does not collect, receive, share, rent, or sell personal information or gameplay information. There are no advertising, analytics, authentication, payment, or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

Children's privacy

Dovetail Words is a general-audience word puzzle intended for teens and adults and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Temporary share images are stored in the app's cache and may be removed by Android. Private Android backups are managed through your Android and Google backup settings. Because the developer does not receive your data, there is no developer-held account or database record to delete.

Dictionary material

The word list and definitions are derived from Princeton WordNet 3.0 and bundled with the app under its license. The app does not contact Princeton University or WordNet while running.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated before the changed version is released.
